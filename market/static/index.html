WHAT THIS DOES — AND WHAT IT PROVES

A transparent starting point.

The baseline compares the latest completed hourly close with 24-hour and 72-hour averages. Aligned upward means bullish; aligned downward means bearish; mixed means neutral. In simulations, bullish holds spot TAO and other conditions hold cash. No shorting, leverage, or wallet connection.

Research needs continuous, timestamped market candles. Missing or stale data produces no clear signal. Historical simulation uses a one-hour execution delay and charges fees and slippage on both entry and exit.

Paid access is not open. This preview has no checkout or customer accounts. A validated data feed, forward results, and subscription controls must be in place before a paid launch.
